The Sphere

Ready to play? 🔮

No controller, no keyboard — just your hands. Everything you do to the sphere, you do in the air.

  • 🤏 Pinch thumb to fingertip to draw — unpinch to move between letters
  • 👉 Point without pinching and the sphere gets out of the way
  • ✊ Curl your hand slowly — the sphere shrinks; open slowly — it grows
  • 💥 Throw your hand open all at once — it bursts
  • 🙌 Show two hands — two spheres; spread to grow, together to collide
  • 👍 Stick your thumb out — particles line up along it and follow it
